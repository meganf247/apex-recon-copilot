2 · Why this matters to Apex

  • Apex sits in the middle of the money: DSPs pay Apex, and Apex pays publishers (~84% of revenue; Apex keeps a ~16% take rate). When a DSP pays on a lower count than Apex owes the publisher, Apex eats the difference.
  • Q3 2026: $428M gross CTV revenue; $27.9M (6.5%) not matched by DSP reports.
  • 921 deal-months across 39 of 40 DSPs needed review in Q3, about 360 new disputes every month.
  • Disputed invoices sit unpaid. At ~$250K of disputed revenue a day, every day of resolution time is working capital locked up.

4 · Today vs. tomorrow

Today: download 40 partner portals’ files in 3 formats → VLOOKUPs → email back and forth → guess the cause → write off what’s left.

Tomorrow: 1. Partner files are ingested and validated automatically (Lakeflow). 2. Every deal is reconciled daily and monthly against Apex logs. 3. A model explains why each gap happened, and a draft dispute is ready (AI). 4. Analysts work one prioritized queue and resolve in one click (Databricks App + Lakebase). 5. Finance asks questions in plain English (Genie).

7 · For the CFO

  • More cash, sooner: ≈ $19M a year of recoverable revenue that currently goes stale, plus faster collection on disputed invoices.
  • Audit-ready: every number traces from the partner file to the invoice (Unity Catalog lineage). Every analyst action is logged (who, what, when, model vs final cause).
  • Controls: revenue figures are visible to Finance only; partner managers see only their own DSPs. Enforced by the platform, not by the app.
  • A forecastable close: daily reconciliation means no month-end surprise.

8 · For the Director of Billing Operations

  • One queue, ranked by dollars, so the team works the right cases first.
  • Root cause on first look 97% of the time, vs 47% with today’s rules of thumb. Analysts confirm or override, and that feedback retrains the model.
  • Dispute emails drafted from the evidence: 250 drafts, every dollar figure checked against the source, rated 5/5 for accuracy and tone by an independent AI judge.
  • Bad partner files caught on arrival (unknown deals, duplicates, bad dates, negative counts, wrong seat), not at month-end.

9 · How it works: one governed platform

Need Databricks
Ingest 3 partner formats + SSP logs, validate Lakeflow declarative pipelines, Auto Loader, data-quality expectations
Govern revenue data by partner and role Unity Catalog: row filters, column masks, tags, lineage
Explain the gap, draft the dispute ML (MLflow, model registry) + GenAI (ai_query, Claude)
Live case workflow Lakebase (Postgres) synced with the lakehouse
Plain-English questions Genie
The analyst’s workbench Databricks App

No data leaves the platform, and there’s no separate operational database or BI tool to stitch together.
